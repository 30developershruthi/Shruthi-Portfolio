About Me

Hello there! My name is Shruthi and I am a Grade 12 IBCP student at Assumption College Catholic School. I am a passionate leader and learner, interested in leadership, technology, and international relations. I enjoy leading various clubs and helping make an impact in my community. Some of the activities I enjoy include coding, debating, playing tennis, and guitar. During my free time, I enjoy catching up on global affairs news as well as watching Shark Tank!
